# Retail Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using Google Sheets to understand sales performance and customer purchasing patterns.

## Objectives
- Calculate total sales and other key performance indicators.
- Analyze sales by product category.
- Compare sales by gender.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Summarize important business findings.

## Tools Used
- Google Sheets
- Pivot Tables
- Spreadsheet Formulas

## Workbook Structure
1. Raw Data – Original retail sales dataset.
2. Working – Data calculations and formulas.
3. Summary – Key performance indicators and findings.
4. Category Sales Pivot – Sales by product category.
5. Gender Sales Pivot – Sales by gender.
6. Month Sales Pivot – Monthly sales analysis.

## Key Findings
- Total Sales: 456,000
- Total Transactions: 1,000
- Total Quantity Sold: 2,514
- Average Transaction Amount: 456
- Highest-Selling Category: Electronics, with sales of 156,905.
- Female Customer Sales: 232,840
- Male Customer Sales: 223,160

## Conclusion
The analysis identifies the best-performing product category and compares sales contributions by gender and month. These findings can help businesses understand sales performance and support better decisions.
